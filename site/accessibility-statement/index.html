The short version

  • This site is partially compliant with WCAG 2.2 Level AA. The remaining items are being fixed now.
  • We tested every public page by hand and with automated tools.
  • If anything gets in your way, email info@astraealink.com and we’ll help.

1.Status

Current status

Partially compliant with WCAG 2.2 Level AA

In W3C terms, the site is “partially conformant”: some parts do not yet fully meet the standard.

We work to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, Level AA. Our November 15, 2026 audit found a small number of items still to fix. We are fixing them in the site’s code and will publish updated results here after re-testing.

3.Scope and method

Pages: Home, About, Plans, Contact, Terms of Use, Privacy Policy and Cookies Policy, in dark and light themes, on desktop and mobile widths.

By hand: keyboard-only use, screen reader structure, 200% zoom and 320-pixel reflow, text spacing, reduced motion, form errors and color contrast.

Tools: WAVE, axe-core and HTML_CodeSniffer, with every flagged item checked by hand.

5.Adjust This Site panel

The panel lets you change how this site looks and moves. It doesn’t make a website accessible or compliant. That takes fixing the code, which is what we do. All results above were tested with the panel switched off.

6.Get help or report a barrier

If you can’t use any part of this site, or need information in another format, email info@astraealink.com or use our contact page. Tell us the page and what happened. We aim to reply within 2 business days, and we’ll give you the information or complete your request another way while we fix the problem.

7.Legal note

The U.S. Department of Justice has not set a technical web standard for private businesses under the Americans with Disabilities Act, and refers to WCAG as helpful guidance. We use WCAG 2.2 Level AA as our standard. This statement describes a self-evaluation on the date shown; it is not legal advice or a guarantee of compliance with any law. See our Terms of Use, Section 4.
