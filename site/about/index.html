Why We Exist

Accessibility Is Fixed in the Code, Not Covered Up.

People use websites with screen readers, keyboards, voice control and zoom. When a button has no name or a checkout traps the keyboard, they can't finish what they came to do, and the business loses them.

Too often, businesses are sold a quick add-on that promises compliance. No tool placed on top of a website can guarantee that. In 2025 one overlay vendor agreed to pay $1 million to settle Federal Trade Commission allegations that it misrepresented its tool's ability to make any website WCAG-compliant (read the FTC announcement).

We started Astraea Link to do the work properly: test by hand, fix the barriers at the source, and prove each fix.

Our Commitments

Six Promises, and How You Can Check Each One.

A promise is only worth something if you can verify it. Each one below comes with the proof you'll have in hand.

  1. 01

    Scope in Writing, Before Any Work

    You get a written quote and contract that list the pages, the deliverables and the delivery dates. Nothing starts until you've signed and made a payment.

    How You Can Check
    It's in your quote and contract, before you pay anything.

  2. 02

    Delivery Dates in Writing

    Every quote names its delivery dates, and we plan our work to meet them. If anything puts a date at risk, on our side or yours, we tell you as soon as we know, before the date, with the reason and a new plan.

    How You Can Check
    Compare each delivery against the dates in your quote.

  3. 03

    Tested by Hand, Not Just Scanned

    We test with a keyboard, a screen reader such as NVDA, zoom and real forms, alongside automated tools. Automated tools can't decide accessibility on their own; the W3C says they can only assist.

    How You Can Check
    Your report covers keyboard, screen reader, zoom, forms, contrast and motion checks.

  4. 04

    Every Issue Tied to WCAG 2.2

    Every barrier we report names the WCAG 2.2 success criterion it fails, where it is on your site and how serious it is. No vague scores.

    How You Can Check
    Look up any criterion in your report on the W3C's WCAG 2.2 page.

  5. 05

    Fixed in Your Code, Then Re-Tested

    On the Accessibility Suite, fixes go into your site's own code, not a widget on top, and every fix is checked again by hand before we sign it off. On our audit plans, you get the fix guidance your developers need.

    How You Can Check
    Suite progress reports list each fix with its re-test result.

  6. 06

    Straight Answers, Even Unwelcome Ones

    We tell you what we found, what's fixed and what's still open. If something is outside what we do, or we're not the right fit, we'll say so on the first call.

    How You Can Check
    Your report lists every issue we found, ranked by severity.

Where We Draw the Line

What We Will and Won't Do.

Knowing what a company refuses to do tells you as much as what it sells.

We Will

  • Give you a written scope, price and timeline before you pay
  • Test by hand as well as with automated tools
  • Tie every issue to a WCAG 2.2 success criterion
  • Fix barriers in your code and re-test every fix (Accessibility Suite)
  • Tell you early if a date is at risk

We Won't

  • Sell an overlay or widget as a compliance fix
  • Promise you'll never be sued. No one honestly can.
  • Sell "ADA certified" badges or "100% compliant" scores
  • Post testimonials, reviews or client logos that aren't real
  • Give legal advice. We're accessibility specialists, not lawyers.
