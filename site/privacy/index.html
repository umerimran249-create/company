Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how United Shah Corporation, doing business as Astraea Link, collects, uses and shares personal information through astraealink.com (the "Site").

1. Information we collect

Information you give us: your name, email address, company name, website address and anything else you choose to include when you book a call, fill in a contact form or email us.

Information collected automatically: like most websites, our hosting provider may record basic technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you visit, for security and to keep the Site running. We do not currently use analytics or advertising tracking tools.

2. How we use it

We use your information to respond to you, schedule and hold calls, review the website you ask us to look at, provide services you request, send you information you have asked for, and keep the Site secure. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising.

3. Who we share it with

We share personal information only with service providers that help us run the business, and only as needed.

Provider Purpose
Calendly Booking and scheduling calls
Microsoft (Outlook / Microsoft 365) Business email
GoDaddy Hosting the Site
Payment processor Processing invoice payments, for clients only

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect our rights, or as part of a sale or reorganization of the business.

4. Cookies and Do Not Track

The Site may use cookies that are needed for it to work. Our scheduling tool may set its own cookies when you use it. We do not use cookies for advertising or cross-site tracking. Because we do not track visitors across websites, the Site does not respond differently to browser "Do Not Track" signals. Third parties do not collect information about your activity across other websites through our Site.

5. How long we keep it

We keep contact and booking information for as long as needed to respond to you and maintain our business relationship, and client records for as long as required for legal, tax and accounting purposes. We delete or anonymize information we no longer need.

6. Your choices

You can ask us to tell you what personal information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it, by emailing ali@astraealink.com. You can unsubscribe from any email we send by replying to it or using the unsubscribe link. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect personal information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children

The Site is for businesses and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top shows when it was last changed. If we make a significant change, we will post a notice on the Site.

10. Contact

Questions or requests about your information: info@astraealink.com
