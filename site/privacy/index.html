The short version

This summary is for convenience only. If it differs from the full Privacy Policy below, the full policy applies.

  • We collect what you send us (name, email, company, website, notes) and basic technical logs our host keeps for security.
  • We use it to reply, schedule calls, review your website and deliver the work you hire us for.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising or cross-site tracking tools.
  • We share it only with the service providers in Section 4, or when the law requires.
  • You can ask to see, correct or delete your information at info@astraealink.com.

1.Who we are

This website, astraealink.com (the "Site"), is operated by United Shah Corporation, a California corporation doing business as Astraea Link ("Astraea Link," "we," "us"). We provide website accessibility audits and code remediation services to businesses.

This Privacy Policy covers personal information we collect through the Site, through email, calls and meetings, and while providing our services. It does not cover third-party websites we link to, which have their own policies.

When a client hires us, our signed Service Agreement also governs how we handle that client's information. If the Service Agreement and this policy conflict on a client matter, the Service Agreement controls.

2.Information we collect

Information you give us

When you book a call, send a form, email us or become a client, we collect your name, email address, company name, job title, phone number if you provide it, your website address, your preferred meeting time, and anything else you choose to include. For clients we also collect billing contact details and project records.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider may record basic technical information, such as your IP address, browser type, device type, the pages you visit and the time of your visit. This is used to run and secure the Site.

Information from other sources

We may collect business contact information (such as a name, job title, company and work email or phone number) from public sources, such as company websites and professional directories, to contact businesses about our services. You can ask us to stop contacting you at any time (see Section 9).

Information we do not need

We do not ask for sensitive personal information such as government ID numbers, health information or precise location. Our audits test your website the way a visitor would; we do not need access to your customers' personal information. If a project ever requires access to systems that contain personal information, that access is governed by our Service Agreement.

3.How we use it

  • To reply to you and to schedule and hold calls.
  • To review websites you ask us to look at and prepare quotes.
  • To provide, bill for and support the services you hire us for.
  • To send you information you asked for and, where allowed, occasional business emails about our services.
  • To keep the Site and our systems secure and to prevent fraud or misuse.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations and to protect our legal rights.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

5.Cookies, local storage and tracking

The Site uses only what it needs to work:

  • Your settings. Your browser's local storage remembers your theme, your "Adjust This Site" options and your cookie choice. This stays on your device and is not sent to us.
  • Hosting and security. Our hosting provider may set cookies needed to deliver and protect the Site.
  • Scheduling. When you use the booking calendar on our Book a Free Call page, Calendly may set its own cookies under its own privacy policy.

We do not use analytics, advertising, session-recording or cross-site tracking tools, and we do not allow third parties to collect information about your activity across other websites through the Site.

You can review your choice at any time with the "Cookie settings" link in the footer.

Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control

Because we do not track you across websites, there is nothing for a Do Not Track signal to turn off, and the Site does not change its behavior in response to one. If we ever add analytics or advertising tools, we will update this policy first and will treat a Global Privacy Control signal from your browser as a request to opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information.

6.Calls and recordings

We do not record calls or video meetings unless we tell you at the start and everyone on the call agrees. If you do not agree, we will not record.

7.Emails from us

You can stop business emails from us at any time by using the unsubscribe link or replying "unsubscribe." We will process your request within 10 business days. We may still send messages about a service you have hired us for, such as invoices and project updates.

8.How long we keep it

We keep contact and booking information for as long as we need it to respond to you and maintain our business relationship. We keep client records for as long as legal, tax or accounting rules require. When we no longer need information, we delete it or anonymize it.

9.Your choices and rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal information we hold about you and give you a copy;
  • correct information that is wrong;
  • delete your information; and
  • stop contacting you about our services.

To make a request, email info@astraealink.com with the subject "Privacy request." We may need to confirm your identity before acting on it, and we will use the information you give us only for that purpose. Someone you authorize may make a request for you if they show us your written permission.

We will respond within 30 days. If we cannot fully meet a request, for example because the law requires us to keep certain records, we will tell you why. We will not treat you differently for making a request.

If a state privacy law gives you additional rights and applies to us, we will honor those rights.

10.Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards to protect personal information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a security breach affects your personal information, we will notify you as required by law.

11.Children

The Site is for businesses and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

12.Where your information is processed

We are based in the United States and serve U.S. businesses. Your information is processed in the United States and in other places where our service providers operate.

13.Accessibility of this policy

We build this page to meet WCAG 2.2 Level AA. If you have trouble reading it, or need it in another format such as plain text or large print, email info@astraealink.com and we will provide it.

14.Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top shows when it last changed. If we make a significant change, we will post a notice on the Site before the change takes effect and, where we have your email and the change affects you, we will email you.
