    Common Questions

    Straight Answers Before You Book.

    Does the ADA apply to my website?

    The U.S. Department of Justice says the ADA’s requirements for businesses open to the public include their websites. It hasn’t set a specific technical standard for private businesses, so courts and settlements usually measure websites against WCAG.

    Whether and how the ADA applies to your business is a legal question, so please speak with an attorney. Read the DOJ’s web accessibility guidance.

    Is WCAG the law?

    WCAG is a technical standard published by the W3C, not a law in itself. For state and local governments, the DOJ’s 2024 rule adopts WCAG 2.1 Level AA, with compliance dates in April 2027 and April 2028.

    For private businesses there’s no codified standard yet, but WCAG is the benchmark most often used. We test to WCAG 2.2 AA, which builds on 2.1 and adds newer requirements.

    Will an accessibility widget or overlay make my site compliant?

    No. No tool added on top of a website can guarantee that. In 2025 the FTC ordered one overlay vendor to pay $1 million over claims that its product could make any website WCAG-compliant. Read the FTC announcement.

    Lasting accessibility comes from fixing the code itself, which is what we do. The “Adjust This Site” panel on our own site is a comfort setting for visitors, not a compliance tool.

    Can you guarantee we won’t be sued?

    No, and no one honestly can. Anyone can file a complaint, and websites change over time. What we can do is find and fix the barriers that lawsuits typically point to, and give you a written record of the work.

    Which plan is right for me?

    Sprint Audit if you need a fast picture of where you stand. Extensive Audit if your own developers will make the fixes and need a roadmap. Accessibility Suite if you want us to fix, re-test and monitor for 12 months, with an assigned project manager and developer.

    Not sure? Book a free call and we’ll recommend one. Compare the plans above.

    Can my own developers fix the issues?

    Yes. The Extensive Audit includes a remediation roadmap with the WCAG criterion, code-level fix and effort estimate for each issue, plus a debrief call with our team to walk through it.

    How much does it cost?

    It depends on your site’s size and which plan fits. Every plan is scoped to your site, and you’ll get a written quote and contract before any work begins. The first call is free.

    How long does an audit take?

    It depends on the size and complexity of your site. We’ll give you a timeline after a short call, before any work begins.

    Is your audit legal advice?

    No. We’re not a law firm, and our audits are technical assessments, not legal opinions. If you’ve received a demand letter or lawsuit, please speak with a qualified attorney.
