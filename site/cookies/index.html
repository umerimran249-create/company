The short version

This summary is for convenience only. If it differs from the full policy below, the full policy applies.

  • Astraea Link doesn't set cookies on visitors' devices.
  • Your theme and "Adjust This Site" settings are saved in your browser only. They're never sent to us.
  • We don't use analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, session recording or cross-site tracking.
  • Details you send through our booking or contact form are covered by our Privacy Policy.

1.Who we are

This website, astraealink.com (the "Site"), is operated by United Shah Corporation, a California corporation doing business as Astraea Link ("we", "us"). This Cookies Policy explains how the Site uses cookies and similar browser storage. It's part of our Privacy Policy.

2.Cookies

Cookies are small files a website saves in your browser. We don't set any cookies when you visit the Site.

Our hosting provider, GoDaddy, may set technical cookies that are strictly needed to deliver the Site and protect it from attacks. These aren't used to identify you or follow you to other websites.

3.Browser storage we use

To remember your display choices between visits, the Site saves them in your browser's local storage. Local storage works like a cookie, but it stays on your device and isn't sent to us or anyone else.

What the Site saves in your browser
ItemWhyHow long it stays
ThemeRemembers whether you chose the light or dark themeUntil you change it or clear your browser data
"Adjust This Site" settingsRemembers the display and motion options you pickedUntil you reset them or clear your browser data

The "Adjust This Site" panel is a comfort tool. It changes how the Site looks and moves for you. It doesn't make any website accessible or compliant.

4.What we don't use

We don't use analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, session recording or cross-site tracking tools. We don't allow other companies to collect information about your activity on the Site, and we don't sell or share your personal information for advertising.

5.Booking and contact forms

When you book a call or use our contact form, you choose to send us your full name, email address, preferred meeting date and time, and any notes you add. This isn't stored in a cookie. It's saved in our website's admin system, hosted by GoDaddy, which only our sales team can access.

We keep these records for up to 24 months after our last contact with you, unless you become a client or ask us to delete them sooner. Our Privacy Policy explains how we use this information and how to ask us to delete it.

6.Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control

Some browsers can send a "Do Not Track" or "Global Privacy Control" signal. We don't track visitors over time or across other websites, so there's no tracking for these signals to turn off, and the Site works the same way whether or not your browser sends them.

7.Your choices

You can clear or block cookies and local storage at any time in your browser's settings, usually under "Privacy" or "Site data". If you do, the Site will still work. It will just forget your theme and "Adjust This Site" choices.

8.Changes to this policy

If we ever add a cookie or a tool that collects information about how you use the Site, we'll update this page before it goes live. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when this policy last changed.

9.Contact and accessibility

Questions about this policy: info@astraealink.com.

We build this page to meet WCAG 2.2 Level AA. If you have trouble reading it, or need it in another format such as plain text or large print, email us and we'll provide it.
